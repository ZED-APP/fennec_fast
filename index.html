Introduction

At Fast Mental Math, we respect users’ privacy and are committed to protecting personal data.

Information We Collect

  • Name or username
  • Email address
  • Account type (Trainer / Trainee)
  • The trainee's date of birth

Children & Family Policy

This application is not intended for children under the age of 13.

  • No data is knowingly collected from children under 13.
  • If discovered, the data is deleted immediately.
  • No advertising, tracking, or profiling of children.
❗ We do not collect or store user photos.

Training Data

  • Training settings sent by trainers
  • Training results (speed, accuracy, exercises)

Account Deletion

Users can delete their account at any time directly from the profile page inside the application. Once deleted, all associated data is permanently removed from our systems.
