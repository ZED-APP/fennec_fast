Request Data Deletion

Zed Soroban respects your privacy. You can request the deletion of part or all of your personal data without deleting your account.

  • User progress and activity data
  • Stored preferences
  • Temporary learning data

To request data deletion, please contact us using the email below and specify what data you want to remove.

📧 Email: support@zedsoroban.com
